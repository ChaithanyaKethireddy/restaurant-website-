Our Story

A Passion for Good Food

Founded in 2012, Savory Table began as a small family kitchen with a simple belief: great food brings people together. Today we serve breakfast, lunch, and dinner — every dish prepared from scratch with ingredients sourced from local farms and markets.

  • ✔ Locally-sourced, seasonal ingredients
  • ✔ Fresh-baked bread & pastries daily
  • ✔ Vegetarian, vegan & gluten-free options
  • ✔ Warm, welcoming atmosphere for every occasion
